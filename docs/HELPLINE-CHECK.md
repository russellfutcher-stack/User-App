# Helpline check

Checked by Max on 29 Oct 2026, against each service's website or current listings found by web search. Nobody has phoned the numbers yet. Before launch, someone should call each one, or confirm it with the service, as the build plan asks.

**Result: all 50 numbers in the app are current.** None needs changing. But 11 lines are not open around the clock, and the app doesn't say so (see "Hours" below).

## Hours

These lines are the only one listed for their country (or the only drugs line) and are closed at some hours:
Ireland (drugs line), France (drugs line), Italy, Denmark, Poland, Portugal, Japan (both), Argentina, Kenya, and possibly South Africa's crisis line.

Done 29 Oct 2026 (agreed by Rusty): the hours are shown next to each of these, in the app and the prototype, and a 24-hour line is added where one exists (Poland: Itaka 800 70 22 22; Kenya: Kenya Red Cross 1199). The emergency number is always shown first. South Africa's crisis line is left as it is, because sources disagree on its hours.
