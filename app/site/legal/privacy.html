Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

User is run by Logical Rules ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who it's shared with and your choices. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Your account: your username and password. The password is stored only as a salted hash, so nobody can read it. If you add a passkey, we store only its public part.
  • Your profile: byline, where you're at, what you're looking for, your interests, and your drug use and mental health choices, with who can see each. Drug use and mental health details are sensitive information; we collect them only because you choose to add them, and you can remove them at any time.
  • Location, if you add it: a city and a country.
  • What you do in User: your chats, room posts, blocks, the reports you make (including who you reported), reports about you, and your reviews and their decisions.
  • Safety records: the decision on each message, and strikes, mutes and pauses.
  • An email address, if you add one, used only to send a code if you forget your password.
  • The country you choose on the Support page, to show help services.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on: a technical address for your device.
  • Technical information: when you last signed in, and the internet address your device uses, which our hosting services see when you connect.

We never ask for your real name or phone number.

How we use it

To run User: to sign you in, match you with others, deliver messages and posts, check them for safety, enforce the community agreement, decide reports and reviews, send notifications and password reset codes, and count members and safety decisions so we can see how User is working.

Who can see what

  • Other members see your username and profile, following your visibility choices; your country; your city only if your setting allows; your room posts; and your messages, if they're in the chat.
  • The arbitrator, the person at Logical Rules who checks safety decisions, can see review cases, reports including who made them, the text of blocked messages (kept for 30 days) and what you write in a review. The arbitrator also gets an email when someone asks for a review, with their username and what they wrote.
  • The person you report is never told who reported them.

Services we use, and where your data goes

  • Supabase stores members' data, in Sydney, Australia.
  • Anthropic, a US company, checks every message, room post, report, review and room request for safety, and writes sample members' replies. It receives the text (and, for sample members, the chat with that sample member), never your username or profile. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. This means message text is processed outside Australia.
  • Resend, a US company, sends emails: password reset codes to you, and alerts to the arbitrator.
  • GitHub, a US company, hosts User's website and app files, and sees your internet address when you open them.
  • If you turn on notifications, your device's notification service (from Apple, Google or your browser's maker) delivers them. Notifications only ever say "You have a new message." or "A review has been decided."

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • The text of messages that weren't sent because they broke the agreement is deleted after 30 days. A record that something was blocked, and why, is kept without it.
  • If you turn on disappearing messages, chat messages are deleted for both of you after 7 days.
  • Password reset codes expire after 15 minutes.
  • Everything else is kept until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete your account, your profile, messages and everything linked to it are erased; we keep only the date, so we can count how many members have left. Copies may remain in our hosting provider's backups for a short time.

Keeping it safe

Connections are encrypted, passwords are hashed, and members can only read their own information and the messages they're part of. Only the arbitrator can see the safety records described above.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your profile, your visibility choices, your location and your reset email on the Me page.
  • Delete your account on the Me page at any time.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or make a complaint: open Support, tap Ask for a review and choose Something else. We'll reply on the Support page within 30 days.
  • If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Age

User is for adults. If there are signs that someone is under 18, their account is paused until they confirm their age, and deleted if they don't confirm within 7 days.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
