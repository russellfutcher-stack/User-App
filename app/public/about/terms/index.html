1. About these terms

User is a peer-support app run by Logical Rules ("we", "us"). These terms are an agreement between you and us. By creating an account or using User, you agree to them and to our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't use User.

2. Who can use User

  • You must be 18 or older. If there are signs that you're under 18, your account is paused until you confirm your age, and it is deleted if you don't confirm within 7 days.
  • Each account is for one person. Keep your password private. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • You don't need to give us your real name, email address or phone number.

3. What User is, and isn't

User is peer support: a place to talk with other people who understand. It is not a medical, counselling, crisis or emergency service, and nothing on User is professional advice. Other members are not health professionals.

If you or someone else is in danger, or someone may have overdosed, call your emergency number now. The Help button at the top of every screen lists help services for your country.

4. The community agreement

Everyone agrees to this when they join:

  1. Be kind. Everyone here is at a different stage, and all of them are welcome.
  2. No buying, selling, sharing or sourcing drugs. No dosing advice.
  3. Stay anonymous. Don't share real names or home addresses, and think carefully before sharing a phone number or your username on another app.
  4. We're peer support, not a crisis service. Help is always at the top.

You must also not: harass, threaten or bully anyone; post hate, sexual content or spam; share another person's private information; pretend to be someone else; or try to get around the safety checks, break the app, or use it for anything illegal.

5. Safety checks

  • Every chat message, room post, report, review and room request is checked automatically before anyone sees it. A message may be delivered, delivered with help information if someone may be at risk, or blocked if it breaks the agreement. If the check can't run, the message isn't sent and you can try again.
  • Breaking the agreement on purpose can earn a strike. Strikes count for 30 days: any strike is a warning; 3 in 7 days is a 24-hour mute; 5 in 30 days is a 7-day pause; 3 for dealing in 7 days pauses your account while it is reviewed, which can lead to removal.
  • You can report another member. You can make one report a day; reports that someone is in danger or under 18 can always be made. The person you report isn't told who reported them.
  • If you think a decision was wrong, you can ask for a review from the Support page. Each decision can be reviewed once. Reviews are decided straight away, and an independent arbitrator checks decisions and can change them. The arbitrator's decision is final.

6. Sample members

Until User has 500 members, it includes sample members to show how it works. They are not real people: each has a "Sample" tag on their profile, their chat replies and room posts are written automatically, and if you ask one whether they're real, they'll tell you. Don't rely on a sample member for support; use the Help button or real members instead.

Mae is an automatic companion you can chat with from the Search page. She is not a real person, and the app says so wherever you meet her. Mae can listen and encourage you, but she isn't a counsellor or a crisis service: in an emergency, use the Help button or call your emergency number.

Links and the names of other apps are allowed, but be careful: anything you do outside User isn't checked or protected by us. Meeting people in person is at your own risk; meet in a public place.
