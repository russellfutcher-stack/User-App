Why people use User

  • Find people who get it. We match you on shared interests, drug use and mental health, so conversations start from common ground.
  • Stay anonymous. No real name, email or phone number. You decide who sees what.
  • Feel less alone, day or night. One-to-one chats, small group rooms, and peer supporters who have been there too.
  • Safe by design. Every message is checked for safety, drug dealing is blocked, and Help now is one tap away.

User is peer support, not a medical or emergency service.

How it works

  1. Create an anonymous account with a username and password.
  2. Set up your profile using the three choices: Interest, Drug use and Mental health, and say if you'd like peer support.
  3. See your matches on the Search page and say hi.
  4. Join rooms on topics that matter to you.
  5. To become a peer supporter, open the Me page and turn on 'Nominate myself as a peer supporter' under Peer support.

You can add User to your phone's home screen: on iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app.

Safety and the community agreement

Everyone agrees to this when they join:

  1. Be kind. Everyone here is at a different stage, and all of them are welcome.
  2. No buying, selling, sharing or sourcing drugs. No dosing advice.
  3. Stay anonymous. Don't share real names, addresses or socials, and think carefully before sharing a phone number.
  4. We're peer support, not a crisis service. Help now is always at the top.

How it's kept safe

  • Every message is checked for safety before anyone sees it. Buying, selling or sourcing drugs, and sharing other apps' contact details, are blocked. Talking about drug use, recovery and harm reduction is always welcome.
  • If a message sounds like someone is in crisis, it's still sent, and they're shown people to talk to straight away.
  • Members can block and report anyone. Serious reports, such as someone being in danger or under 18, are dealt with urgently.
  • Breaking the agreement leads to a warning, then a mute, a pause, or removal.
  • Anyone who thinks a decision was wrong can ask for a review. Every review is decided straight away, and an independent arbitrator checks decisions and can change them.

Privacy policy

User is run by Logical Rules. Questions about your data: russellfutcher@gmail.com.

What we keep

  • Your username and password. Your password is stored only as a salted hash, so nobody can read it.
  • Your profile: byline, stage, what you're looking for, your interest, drug use and mental health choices, and who can see each.
  • Your chats, room posts, blocks, reports and reviews.
  • The country you choose on the Support page, if you choose one.

We never ask for your real name, email address or phone number.

Where it's kept

Members' data is stored in Australia (Sydney).

Safety checks

Every message, room post, report, review and room request is checked for safety before anyone sees it. The check is done by an automated service from Anthropic, a US company, so the text is processed outside Australia. Only the text and where it was sent (a chat or a room) are sent, never your username or profile. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models.

How long we keep things

  • Messages that weren't sent because they broke the community agreement: the text is deleted after 30 days. A record that something was blocked, and why, is kept without it.
  • If you turn on disappearing messages, chat messages are deleted for both of you after 7 days.
  • Everything else is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

Your choices

  • Choose who sees your drug use and mental health choices on the Me page. They start as "People I chat with".
  • Delete your account on the Me page. This permanently erases your profile, messages and everything linked to it.
