Connection and Recovery

A clickable prototype of an anonymous companion app for people who use substances, are in recovery, or live with a mental health condition, and who feel alone.

Everything is built around three choices: Interests, Drug use and Mental health. Sign in or create an account, then pick a choice on Home or the Search page to find people who share it. Help is always one tap away.

Prototype only. All 100 members are fictional sample profiles and replies are simulated. Your data stays in this browser. Demo account: QuietHeron / password quiet heron walks. Arbitrator dashboard: Arbitrator / fair review
